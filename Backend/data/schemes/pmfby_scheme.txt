# PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY) - PADDY RICE GUIDELINES
Source Document: Revised Operational Guidelines for Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India & Kerala State Agricultural Insurance Scheme.

1. Objective:
PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest stages for food crops including Paddy (Rice).

2. Premium Rate:
For Kharif/Autumn and Summer (Punja) Food & Oilseeds crops, the maximum uniform premium payable by farmers is limited to 2.0% of Sum Insured (1.5% for Rabi/Winter crops), with the balance premium subsidized equally by the Central and State Governments.

3. Coverage of Post-Harvest and Inundation Losses:
Coverage is available for up to a maximum period of two weeks (14 days) from harvesting for those crops which are kept in "cut & spread" condition to dry in the field against specific perils of cyclonic and unseasonal rains, and localized inundation/floods in low-lying tracts such as Kuttanad.

4. Claims Settlement Process:
Localized calamity assessments and mid-season adversity relief allow prompt advance payouts of up to 25% of likely claims to assist farmers in mitigating immediate weather-induced financial distress.
