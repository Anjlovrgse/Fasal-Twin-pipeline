# KERALA SUPPLYCO PADDY PROCUREMENT AND STATE INCENTIVE BONUS SCHEME
Source Document: Kerala State Civil Supplies Corporation (Supplyco) Paddy Procurement Operational Manual, Department of Food and Civil Supplies, Government of Kerala.

1. Objective:
To ensure remunerative returns to paddy cultivators across Kerala (especially the Kuttanad and Palakkad paddy belts) through direct institutional procurement at designated Krishibhavan padasekharam collection points.

2. Price and State Incentive Bonus (SIB):
Paddy is procured at the Minimum Support Price (MSP) notified by Government of India plus the Kerala State Incentive Bonus (SIB), delivering an aggregate procurement rate of Rs. 2,820 per quintal (Rs. 28.20 per kg) directly to registered farmers.

3. Payment Mechanism (Paddy Receipt Sheet - PRS Loan):
Upon physical delivery and moisture inspection of paddy (FAQ standards: maximum 17% moisture content), a Paddy Receipt Sheet (PRS) is issued. Under the PRS Loan scheme, partner commercial and cooperative banks disburse immediate payment to farmer accounts, with the State Government servicing the loan interest and principal directly.

4. Procurement Logistics & Mill Allocation:
Procured paddy is directly lifted from farmgate and FPO collection centers by contracted Modern Rice Mills (MRMs), avoiding private middleman mandi deductions and distress sales.
