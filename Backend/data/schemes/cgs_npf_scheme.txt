# CREDIT GUARANTEE SCHEME FOR E-NWR-BASED PLEDGE FINANCING (CGS-NPF)
Source Document: Operational Guidelines for Credit Guarantee Scheme for e-NWR based Pledge Financing, Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (WDRA) & Department of Food and Public Distribution, Government of India.

1. Objective:
The CGS-NPF scheme aims to provide credit guarantee coverage to banks and financial institutions for post-harvest loans extended to farmers, FPOs, and traders against electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWRs) issued by WDRA-registered warehouses, preventing distress sales immediately post-harvest.

2. Guarantee Coverage:
The scheme provides a credit guarantee cover up to 75% to 85% of the outstanding loan amount for pledge financing against e-NWRs, lowering interest rates and reducing collateral requirements for smallholders and FPOs.

3. Eligible Loan Quantum:
Pledge financing loans up to Rs. 75 Lakhs per individual farmer and up to Rs. 5 Crore per FPO/PACS against agricultural produce deposited in registered scientific storage facilities.

4. Mechanism of Liquidity Relief:
Farmers can deposit harvested paddy into accredited warehouses, receive an e-NWR on their mobile device, and avail instant pledge loans from partner commercial/cooperative banks to meet immediate cash needs while awaiting favorable market price realization.
